
# E-Commerce Sales Analysis 

##  Project Overview

The **E-Commerce Sales Analysis Dashboard** is an end-to-end data analytics project designed to analyze retail sales data and provide actionable business insights through interactive dashboards and SQL-based analysis.

The project demonstrates the complete analytics workflow, including data cleaning, data transformation, exploratory analysis, SQL querying, KPI reporting, and dashboard development using Power BI. The objective is to help business stakeholders monitor sales performance, identify profitable products and regions, understand customer purchasing behavior, and make data-driven business decisions.

---

##  Business Problem

Retail businesses generate thousands of sales transactions every day. However, raw transactional data alone cannot provide meaningful insights for strategic decision-making.

The business wanted to answer key questions such as:

- Which product categories generate the highest revenue?
- Which products are the most profitable?
- Which regions contribute the most to sales and profits?
- How do discounts affect profitability?
- Which customer segments drive the most revenue?
- What are the monthly sales and profit trends?
- How can the company improve profitability?

This project addresses these questions by transforming raw sales data into meaningful business insights through analytics and visualization.

##  Key Findings

### Overall Business Performance

- The business generated approximately **$2.29 Million** in total sales.
- Total profit was approximately **$286K**, indicating a positive overall business performance.
- Nearly **10,000 customer orders** were analyzed.

### Category Performance

- **Technology** generated the highest sales and profit among all categories.
- **Office Supplies** maintained stable profitability.
- **Furniture** generated strong sales but comparatively lower profits.

### Observation

Higher sales do not always translate into higher profitability.

### Business Insight

Furniture products require pricing optimization and better discount management to improve profit margins.

---

### Regional Performance

- The **West Region** generated the highest sales and profit.
- The **East Region** was the second-highest contributor.
- The **Central Region** showed relatively lower profitability despite good sales.

### Observation

Regional performance varies significantly across the business.

### Business Insight

Business strategies should be customized based on regional performance rather than applying a single strategy across all regions.

---

### Customer Segment Analysis

- The **Consumer** segment contributed the largest share of total sales.
- **Corporate** customers generated stable profits.
- **Home Office** customers contributed comparatively lower revenue.

### Observation

Consumer customers represent the company's largest customer base.

### Business Insight

Customer retention and personalized marketing campaigns should primarily target Consumer customers.

---

### Product Performance

Top-performing sub-categories included:

- Copiers
- Phones
- Accessories
- Paper
- Binders

Loss-making sub-categories included:

- Tables
- Bookcases
- Supplies

### Observation

Some products generate high revenue but consistently produce losses.

### Business Insight

Pricing strategies and procurement costs for low-performing products should be reviewed.

---

### Discount Analysis

The analysis showed that:

- Higher discounts generally reduced profitability.
- Moderate discounts improved sales without significantly impacting profits.
- Excessive discounts resulted in negative profit margins for certain products.

### Observation

Discounts increase sales volume but reduce overall profitability.

### Business Insight

The business should implement controlled discount policies based on product profitability.

---

### Monthly Sales Trends

Monthly analysis revealed seasonal fluctuations in both sales and profit.

### Observation

Certain months consistently generated higher revenue compared to others.

### Business Insight

Marketing campaigns and inventory planning should align with seasonal demand patterns.

---

### Shipping Performance

Shipping efficiency was evaluated using shipping duration and shipping modes.

### Observation

Different shipping modes showed varying delivery times.

### Business Insight

Optimizing shipping methods can improve customer satisfaction and operational efficiency.

---

## 📌 Key Insights

- Technology products contribute the highest profitability.
- Furniture products require pricing optimization.
- Consumer customers generate the largest revenue.
- High discount levels significantly reduce profit margins.
- The West region is the strongest-performing market.
- A small group of products contributes the majority of overall profit.
- Sales exhibit seasonal patterns that can guide future planning.

---

##  Business Recommendations

### 1. Optimize Discount Strategy

Reduce excessive discounting on low-margin products to improve profitability.

### 2. Prioritize High-Profit Products

Increase marketing efforts for high-margin products such as Technology products and Copiers.

### 3. Improve Furniture Profitability

Review pricing strategies, supplier costs, and promotional offers for Furniture products.

### 4. Strengthen Regional Strategies

Expand operations and marketing efforts in high-performing regions while improving performance in underperforming regions.

### 5. Focus on Customer Retention

Develop loyalty programs targeting Consumer customers, who contribute the highest revenue.

### 6. Improve Inventory Planning

Use seasonal sales trends to optimize inventory management and reduce stock shortages.
